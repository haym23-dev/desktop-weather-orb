Detailed Description

Owns the NeoPixel strand and the precomputed geometry of every LED.

This class knows nothing about weather; it just hands out an RGB framebuffer and the LED positions for a renderer (see weather_sunny.h) to fill in, then pushes that buffer to the strip on show().
