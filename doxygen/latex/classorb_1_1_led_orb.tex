\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Owns the Neo\+Pixel strand and the precomputed geometry of every LED.

This class knows nothing about weather; it just hands out an RGB framebuffer and the LED positions for a renderer (see weather\+\_\+sunny.\+h) to fill in, then pushes that buffer to the strip on \doxylink{classorb_1_1_led_orb_a9f4ca05a01d993a16691e320292a2ea7}{show()}.
