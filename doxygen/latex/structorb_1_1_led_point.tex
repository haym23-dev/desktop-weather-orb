\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A single addressable LED\textquotesingle{}s position on the orb.

Directions are unit vectors in a right-\/handed frame with +Z pointing up (from the base of the orb toward its top):

+Z = up, the "{}zenith"{} of the sky -\/Z = down, the base +X = the 0 rad azimuth reference

{\ttfamily \doxylink{structorb_1_1_led_point_a23ef61a5f121987185e4998806e715d2}{azimuth}} is how far the strand has wrapped around the vertical axis (it grows past 2\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}pi as the helix climbs), and {\ttfamily \doxylink{structorb_1_1_led_point_ab532df4ba8f476ffcd138c110dcdd0cc}{altitude}} is a cheap 0..1 scalar for the vertical position (0 = bottom pole, 1 = top pole) used by the sky gradients.
